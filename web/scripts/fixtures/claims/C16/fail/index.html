Freezing a system app

Thor asks Android to switch the package off and leave it where it is, and your app data stays where it is. Some vendors refuse that for their own preinstalled apps, and where they do, Thor falls back to removing the app so the freeze still happens. A Shizuku freeze switches the app off or removes it for your user, whichever the platform will allow. The command underneath is pm uninstall -k, which is the flag that keeps the data directories.

A system app a freeze removed for your Android user is gone from the launcher until you put it back. Where the disable is not exposed, a Shizuku freeze removes it for your user instead.
