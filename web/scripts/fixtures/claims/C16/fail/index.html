Freezing a system app

Thor asks Android to switch the package off and leave it where it is, and your app data stays where it is. Some vendors refuse that for their own preinstalled apps, and where they do, Thor falls back to removing the app so the freeze still happens. Under Shizuku it switches the app off or removes it for your user, whichever the platform will allow. The command underneath is pm uninstall -k, which is the flag that keeps the data directories.
