Freezing a system app

Thor asks Android to switch the package off and leave it where it is. That is the only thing a freeze does, in every privilege mode, and your app data stays where it is because nothing was taken away to begin with.

Some vendors refuse that for their own preinstalled apps. Where they do, Thor tells you the freeze failed and leaves the app alone, which is the honest answer even though it is the less useful one. Earlier builds substituted a removal there, and that was withdrawn: a package taken away that way reads as gone to everything that does not go looking for it, and standing in for a switch-off with something that drastic was never a substitution to make on your behalf without saying so.
