📦 **Thor v1.95.1 — Backup & Restore Hub, Action Customization & XAPK**

Cumulative stable release — everything since v1.94.0.

**✨ New:**

• 📦 **Backup & Restore Hub** — find, restore, share and delete `.thorbak`, `.xapk`, `.apks` and `.apk` archives from one screen.
• 💾 **Encrypted App Data Backup** — offline AES-256-GCM `.thorbak`: full app data on root, APK + shared storage on Shizuku.
• ⚙️ **Customizable Actions** — drag to reorder or hide App Info actions, preview pinned while you rearrange.
• 👆 **Icon Gestures** — tap an app's icon to open it, long-press for its settings.
• 🎮 **Full .xapk & OBB Support**, and **8 languages**: EN, AR, ES, FR, PL, PT, PT-BR, ZH.

**🛠 Fixed:** app lists showing only Thor on Chinese ROMs and fresh installs; newly granted root or Dhizuku applies on Refresh, no restart; clearer freezer action labels.
